01How long is the Black Friday offer on?+

Until midnight at the end of Cyber Monday, 30 November. When the timer hits zero, prices go back to normal.

02What is the Lifetime Team?+

It's the annual membership: a full year of Shape Up for £149, half the price of paying £24.99 a month for 12 months. That works out at about 41p a day.

03What exactly do I get with the membership?+

Everything: all gym and home training plans, every class, nutrition guidance and meal ideas, the barcode scanner, calorie and macro tracking, habit and cycle trackers, the member community and the weekly live with Gabby. New content is added every month at no extra cost, and there is no higher tier to upgrade to.

04Can I cancel whenever I want?+

Yes. You can cancel in two taps inside the app, with no email or phone call needed.

05I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?+

Yes. Every exercise has a demo video, sessions start from 20 minutes, and your suggested weights are based on what you actually lifted last week.

06Do I need a gym membership?+

No. Every plan comes with gym and home options, so you can train wherever suits you.

07How much time do I need each week?+

Plans run 3 to 5 days a week, with sessions from 20 minutes.

08Do I still need a separate food tracking app?+

No. The barcode scanner, full UK food database and calorie and macro tracking are all built into Shape Up.

09Will the plans work around my cycle?+

Yes. Shape Up has a built-in cycle tracker, so you can log your cycle right alongside your workouts, habits and nutrition. Energy, strength and recovery can change through the month, so on lower energy days you can lighten the weights, swap to a home session or pick a gentler class, then push harder on the days you feel strong. Your plan stays on track, you just adjust how you train to suit how you feel.

10What if I get stuck or need help?+

Ask in the member community, join Gabby's weekly live, or email support@shapeupwithgabby.co.uk and you'll hear back within a day.
